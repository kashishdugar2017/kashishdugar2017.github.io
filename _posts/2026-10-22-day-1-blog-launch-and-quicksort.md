## Grokking Chapter 4 — QUICKSORT

Quicksort = Divide and Conquer!

Steps:
1. Pick a PIVOT
2. Smaller numbers to LEFT, bigger to RIGHT
3. Do the same on each side (RECURSION!)
4. Base case: 0 or 1 item is already sorted

Big-O: O(n log n) — BEATS selection sort O(n²)!

I played the quicksort game by hand with paper (sorted 3, 8, 15, 19, 22, 31, 47) and then wrote it in Python. The output matched my paper game perfectly.

## Petzold Chapter 4 — Anatomy of a Flashlight

A flashlight has 4 parts: battery, wire, bulb, switch.

- Conductor = carries electricity (copper, which I found this morning in my jumper wire!)
- Insulator = blocks electricity (rubber, plastic)
- Switch ON (closed) = light ON
- Switch OFF (open) = light OFF

BIG IDEA: A flashlight is binary. ON or OFF. Just like Morse (dot/dash), Braille (bump/flat), and binary (0/1). Computers are just millions of tiny flashlights!
