## Dijkstra's algorithm
BFS finds the path with the fewest steps, but fewest steps is not always cheapest. Dijkstra's algorithm finds the cheapest path when roads have different costs.

You keep two tables: costs (how much it costs to get there from Start) and parents (where you came from). You always process the cheapest place next. Once it's processed, its cost is final.

With help, I worked through the book's example and the cheapest path was 6: Start → B → A → Finish.
